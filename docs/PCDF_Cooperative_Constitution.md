# Constitution of the UP Dairy Cooperative Structure
## Farmer → Samiti (DCS) → Sangh / Milk Union → PCDF

*Compiled from the official PCDF website (updairydevelopment.gov.in), the NDDB Dairy Knowledge Portal, and the Uttar Pradesh Co-operative Societies Act, 1965. Date: 05 July 2026.*

---

## 1. The Big Picture

PCDF (Pradeshik Cooperative Dairy Federation Ltd., Lucknow) was established in **1962** to develop organised dairying in Uttar Pradesh on cooperative lines and to eliminate middlemen between milk producers and consumers. It follows the **Anand Pattern three-tier structure**:

```
                    ┌─────────────────────────────┐
   STATE LEVEL      │  PCDF (Apex Federation)     │  Markets milk & products
                    │  "Parag" brand              │  (Apex Society, Sec 2(a-4))
                    └──────────────▲──────────────┘
                                   │ Unions are shareholder-members;
                                   │ Union chairmen sit on Federation Board
                    ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
   DISTRICT LEVEL   │  Dugdh Utpadak Sahkari      │  Collects, processes,
                    │  Sangh = MILK UNION         │  markets milk
                    │  (one per district/region)  │  (Central Society, Sec 2(d-1))
                    └──────────────▲──────────────┘
                                   │ Samitis are shareholder-members;
                                   │ Samiti delegates elect Union Board
                    ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
   VILLAGE LEVEL    │  Dugdh Utpadak Sahkari      │  Collects surplus milk
                    │  Samiti = Dairy Cooperative │  twice daily, tests, pays
                    │  Society (DCS)              │  (Primary Society)
                    └──────────────▲──────────────┘
                                   │ Farmers are shareholder-members;
                                   │ one member = one vote
                    ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
   GRASSROOTS       │  FARMER (Milk Producer)     │  Owner + supplier + voter
                    └─────────────────────────────┘
```

**Important terminology note:** "Sangh" and "Milk Union" are the **same body** — the district-level *Dugdh Utpadak Sahkari Sangh Ltd.* is simply the Hindi name of the District Milk Union. So the chain is: Farmer → Samiti (village) → Sangh/Milk Union (district) → PCDF (state).

Per the official PCDF Three-Tier Structure page: "The DCS in villages collect the surplus milk from farmer members"; "The Milk Unions collect milk from all the village DCS of its district, process and market it"; and "The Apex Federation provides Marketing services and other support to the District Milk Unions of the State."

**Legal framework:** All three tiers are registered cooperative societies governed by the **Uttar Pradesh Co-operative Societies Act, 1965** (UP Act No. 11 of 1966) and the **UP Co-operative Societies Rules, 1968**, each operating under its own registered bye-laws.

## 2. Level 1 — The Farmer (Milk Producer Member)

The farmer is the foundation and the ultimate **owner** of the entire structure.

### How a farmer is constituted as a member
- Any milk producer of the village who has attained majority (18+) is eligible for membership (Sec. 17, UPCS Act permits individuals of majority age as members).
- The farmer becomes a member by **purchasing at least the minimum share(s)** of the Samiti and **paying the entrance fee** prescribed in the Samiti's bye-laws.
- Typical bye-law conditions: the member should reside in the society's area of operation, own a milch animal, and regularly pour milk to the society.

### Rights of the member
- **One member, one vote** in the General Body of the Samiti, regardless of shares held or milk poured — the core cooperative principle.
- Right to elect (and be elected to) the Managing Committee of the Samiti.
- Right to receive payment for milk on quality (fat/SNF) and quantity basis, daily or weekly.
- Right to a share of profits — **dividend on shares and bonus in proportion to milk supplied** during the year.
- Access to input services at cost: artificial insemination (AI), veterinary first aid, cattle feed, fodder seed, cattle insurance.

### Duties of the member
- Pour surplus milk regularly to the Samiti (not to private vendors).
- Abide by the registered bye-laws and General Body resolutions.

## 3. Level 2 — The Samiti (Village Dairy Cooperative Society / DCS)

*Hindi: Prarambhik Dugdh Utpadak Sahkari Samiti — the primary society at village level.*

### Constitution / formation
- Formed when milk producers of a village organise and apply for registration to the **Registrar/Assistant Registrar of Co-operative Societies** (through the Dairy Development Department, which promotes and organises new samitis).
- Under **Section 6** of the UPCS Act, a primary society needs **not less than 10 individual members** to be registered (bye-laws for dairy samitis commonly require more in practice).
- Under **Section 7**, the Registrar registers the society after verifying compliance with the Act, sound business principles, and reasonable prospects of success.
- Each Samiti operates under **registered model bye-laws** which define its area of operation, objects, share capital, membership conditions, and management.

### Governance
- **General Body (all farmer members) is the supreme/final authority** — Sec. 28: "The final authority of a co-operative society shall vest in the general body of its members in general meeting." An **Annual General Meeting** (Sec. 32) approves the accounts, activities, and disposal of net profits.
- **Managing Committee (Prabandh Samiti)**: elected by and from the members. Under Sec. 29 a primary society's committee may have up to **13 members**, including **reserved seats for Scheduled Castes/Tribes, Other Backward Classes, and women**; the elected term is **5 years**, with elections conducted under the UP Co-operative Election Commission.
- A **Chairman (Sabhapati)** is elected from among committee members (Sec. 30) — he exercises "control, supervision and guidance" of the society's affairs and presides over meetings. **All Managing Committee positions are honorary** (unpaid).
- A paid **Secretary** (Sec. 31) acts as chief executive of the society, and the Samiti employs paid staff (milk tester, helper) in proportion to its business.

### Functions
- Twice-daily (morning/evening) **milk collection** from members; weighing and **quality testing** (fat testing by Gerber centrifuge or electronic milkotester) in front of the member; recording of each member's quantity and fat.
- **Dispatch of milk** to the Milk Union via scheduled routes or bulk milk coolers.
- **Payment to members** on quality-cum-quantity basis, daily or weekly.
- **Routing of input services** from the Union to farmers: AI service, liquid nitrogen and semen doses, veterinary first aid and medicines, cattle feed, fodder seed, cattle insurance — on a cost-recovery basis.
- Maintaining daily registers of transactions; accounts subject to **audit**.
- Awareness, training, women's participation, clean milk production, and village development activities.

## 4. Level 3 — The Sangh / District Milk Union

*Hindi: (Jila) Dugdh Utpadak Sahkari Sangh Ltd. — e.g., Varanasi Milk Union, Lucknow Milk Union. This is a "Central Society" under Sec. 2(d-1) of the UPCS Act: a cooperative whose ordinary members are other cooperatives (the samitis), not individuals.*

### Constitution
- All registered **Samitis of the district become members of the Union by subscribing to its share capital** as provided in the Union's bye-laws.
- Registered under the UPCS Act 1965 with its own bye-laws; its area of operation is the district (or a group of districts).

### Governance
- **General Body of the Union** = representatives/delegates (normally the chairmen) of the member Samitis. Sec. 28 allows the final authority to be exercised through elected delegates for federal societies.
- **Board of Directors / Committee of Management**: elected by the Samiti representatives. Under Sec. 29 a central society's committee may have up to **15 members**, with the same SC/ST/OBC/women reservations and a **5-year term**.
- An elected **Union Chairman** presides; day-to-day operations run under **professional paid management** (General Manager/Managing Director and technical staff), keeping the elected board responsible for policy — the Anand Pattern principle of farmer control + professional management.
- Subject to "continuous and concurrent audit to maintain financial propriety."

### Functions
- **Procures milk from all village DCS of the district**, chills, **processes** it in its dairy plant, and **markets** liquid milk and products (largely under the Parag brand through the Federation).
- **Macro-level input supply** through the samitis: compounded cattle feed, fodder seeds, veterinary services for sick animals; owns infrastructure such as dairy plants, cattle-feed plants, fodder farms and semen stations. (PCDF's dairy plants at Noida, Meerut, Lucknow, Kanpur, Allahabad and cattle-feed plants at Meerut and Varanasi are ISO-9002/HACCP certified.)
- **Training** of samiti managing committees, secretaries, AI workers and union staff; extension and rural development work.
- **Financial returns downward**: pays member samitis **dividend on shares and bonus proportional to milk supplied** annually.
- Markets its processed products **through the state federation** (PCDF).

## 5. Level 4 — PCDF (Pradeshik Cooperative Dairy Federation Ltd.)

*The state-level apex body, Lucknow. An "Apex Society" under Sec. 2(a-4) of the UPCS Act — a state-level federation covering the whole of Uttar Pradesh whose members are the central societies (Milk Unions).*

### Constitution
- Established **1962**; registered under the UP Co-operative Societies Act.
- **Member Milk Unions subscribe to PCDF's share capital** as per Federation bye-laws (the State Government also holds share capital as it is a government-supported apex federation).

### Governance
- **General Body** = the member Milk Unions (through their delegates).
- **Board of Directors**: comprises the **elected Chairmen of all member Unions** plus the Federation's **Managing Director**, along with ex-officio (government) members and technical experts. Under Sec. 29, an apex society's committee may have up to **17 members**.
- The **Managing Director** is, per Sec. 31A of the Act, a senior **Government officer (IAS) nominated by the State Government**, serving ex-officio on the board — currently Shri Vaibhav Srivastava, IAS; the department is headed politically by the Minister of Dairy Development (Shri Dharmpal Singh).
- A **Programming Committee** — the chief executives of every member Union plus the Federation's Quality Control Officer and co-opted technical experts — meets regularly to implement Board policies and coordinate day-to-day operations.

### Functions
- **Marketing** of milk and milk products of the member Unions across the state under the **"Parag"** brand; deciding the **product-price mix** for member unions.
- Operating the **state milk grid** — production planning and moving milk between surplus and deficit regions, plus inter-state milk commerce.
- **Liaison with the State/Central Government, NDDB** and other agencies; mobilising resources and coordinating development programmes.
- **Policy-making** for cooperative marketing of all member Unions' milk and products; joint services (AI/breeding programmes, cattle-feed marketing, technical inputs).
- Strengthening the cooperative structure itself (organising new samitis, training, quality assurance through its central labs).
- **Profit distribution** to member Unions equitably, based on the volume of business each Union transacted.

## 6. How Democracy Flows Up and Services Flow Down

**Election chain (bottom-up):**
Farmers elect the Samiti Managing Committee and its Chairman → Samiti chairmen/delegates elect the Union Board of Directors and Union Chairman → Union Chairmen constitute the PCDF Board (with the government-nominated MD). Every officeholder is thus ultimately accountable to the farmer.

**Milk chain (bottom-up):**
Farmer pours milk at the Samiti → Samiti dispatches to the Union's chilling centre/dairy plant → Union processes and packs → PCDF/Parag markets in cities and coordinates the state grid.

**Money & services chain (top-down):**
Consumer payments flow from PCDF → Unions → Samitis → farmer's milk payment (quality-cum-quantity basis), plus year-end dividend/bonus at each tier; while inputs (cattle feed, semen, vet care, training) flow from the Federation and Unions through the Samiti to the farmer's doorstep.

## 7. Quick Reference Table

| Feature | Samiti (Village DCS) | Sangh / Milk Union (District) | PCDF (State) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal status | Primary society | Central society, Sec 2(d-1) | Apex society, Sec 2(a-4) |
| Members | Individual milk producers (min. 10 to register, Sec 6) | Village Samitis (via share capital) | District Milk Unions (via share capital) |
| Supreme authority | General Body of farmers (Sec 28) | General Body of samiti delegates | General Body of member unions |
| Board | Managing Committee ≤ 13, honorary | Board of Directors ≤ 15 | Board ≤ 17: union chairmen + MD (IAS, Sec 31A) + experts |
| Term / elections | 5 years, UP Coop. Election Commission, SC/ST/OBC/women reserved seats | Same | Same |
| Chief executive | Paid Secretary (Sec 31) | Professional GM/MD | Managing Director — State-nominated IAS |
| Core job | Collect, test, pay; route inputs | Process & market; supply inputs; train | Market (Parag), state milk grid, policy, govt/NDDB liaison |
| Returns to members | Milk price + dividend + bonus on milk poured | Dividend + bonus on milk supplied | Profit shared by unions' business volume |
